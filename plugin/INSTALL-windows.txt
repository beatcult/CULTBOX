CULTBOX plugins (Windows, 64 bit): PLVKS and DUBMARINE
Copy:
  cultbox-*.clap   to C:\Program Files\Common Files\CLAP\
  cultbox-*.vst3   to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
Then rescan plugins in your host. Windows SmartScreen or antivirus may warn about the unsigned files; they are built in the open
on GitHub (see the repository's Actions). Instruments; they take notes and MIDI, output stereo.
